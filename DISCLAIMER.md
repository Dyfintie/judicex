# Disclaimer

Judicex is software for legal memory, document organization, evidence
retrieval, workflow checks and drafting assistance. It is a tool, not a
lawyer.

**Judicex does not provide legal advice. Judicex does not establish an
attorney-client relationship. Judicex does not replace review by a
qualified legal professional.**

Outputs may be:

- incomplete (the source set may not contain the relevant rule);
- incorrect (the model may misread or misapply a rule);
- outdated (versioned sources are only as fresh as the last ingestion);
- unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction, court or fact pattern.

The grounded answer engine is designed to **fail closed** — to abstain
rather than invent — but it cannot guarantee that every grounded answer is
correct. Citations link to source documents stored in your local memory;
verifying that the cited text actually supports the claim, in the version
that applies on the date that matters for your case, is your responsibility.

If Judicex is used in the course of a professional engagement, the
professional remains fully responsible for:

- validating every result against authoritative sources;
- exercising independent professional judgment;
- complying with applicable bar rules, professional secrecy, and data
